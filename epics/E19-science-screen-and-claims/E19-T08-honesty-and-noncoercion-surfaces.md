## Goal

The two surfaces where "no promise of certainty" and "no coercion" stop being implicit rules and become **registered, sourced content** on the T07 screen. (1) The **no-promise honesty card**: durable retention is attributed to the **cycle ceiling** (C-016) plus **overlearning** (C-005) — a structural guarantee, never a probability number; chasing a global 0.99 would multiply the daily load and break the sustainable cycle (C-017); and the app says plainly "we can't promise you'll never forget a page" (C-025). The decay ḥadīth (C-035, rendered through the T05 `[TRAD]` variant) and the forgetting curve are framed as the **honest premise of calm loss-prevention, never a threat**. (2) The **no-streaks/no-guilt trust-signal card**: the app deliberately avoids streaks, badges, and guilt because the research is against them (C-041); missing one day doesn't undo your progress (C-042); reminders stay blameless (C-043); progress is your own map, never a ranking (C-044); an act of worship is defined by its intention (C-045, `[TRAD]` via T05) — each a calm T04 row with its named source, turning a constraint into a visible trust signal (11 §6). This task owns only these two content surfaces: the base row is T04, the `[TRAD]` variant mechanics are T05, the screen shell is T07, ARB values are T09, the consolidated goldens are T10.

## Implementation notes

Presentation-only content composition: no engine call, no clock, no network, no new science strings. Every fact on either card is a register row id resolved through the T03 read model; the only strings this task may key are non-factual card titles/framing lines (values authored in T09). The honesty/adab invariants are **copy correctness-critical** → pinned test-first by widget tests + the never-ship banned-phrase lint before any visual polish.

1. **Files** (`features` package, `lib/src/science/widgets/`, per eng-add-feature-module): `honesty_no_promise_card.dart` and `noncoercion_trust_signal_card.dart` — two dumb stateless widgets, each taking a list of pre-resolved register rows (from the T03 provider, by id) and composing T04/T05 rows into one calm card. No state beyond the rows' own expansion state; no `DateTime.now()`; no mutation; no new interactive element of its own.
2. **The no-promise card renders C-016 and C-025 as its two honesty face rows** (plus the C-035 premise row, note 3). C-016's registered headline carries the guarantee in the ḥāfiẓ's frame ("Every page is guaranteed a revision at least once per cycle you choose — the app can only revise it *more* often, never less"); C-025 carries the honest limit ("We can't promise you'll never forget a page…"). C-005 (the permastore plateau that still slopes) and C-017 (why not 0.99) compose as supporting "the evidence" content — detail on demand per the two-layer rule, never a wall of numbers.
3. **Row order tells the honest story: premise → guarantee → limit.** The no-promise card reads top-to-bottom as C-035 (recitation slips by nature — the calm premise), C-016 (so every page is guaranteed a revision within your cycle), C-025 (and still, we can't promise you'll never forget a page) — with C-005/C-017 as the evidence detail beneath. The order itself is adab: the ḥadīth opens as motivation for the guarantee, never as a closing warning.
4. **No percentage, anywhere it could read as a promise.** The collapsed card renders **no** percent glyph (`%` / `٪`) and no numeric retention figure; the only percent-bearing text permitted anywhere is C-017's **registered wording, verbatim**, inside "the evidence" expansion — a number named only to be *rejected* ("we don't chase a '99% retention' number"), carrying its disclosed-simplification caveat. No gauge, dial, meter, progress bar, or slider visualizes retention on either card.
5. **C-016's rule copy is the engine's rule, with no drift.** The value/rule line renders from the register row (which mirrors PRD §7.6: `due_at = min(ideal_due, ceiling_due)`; SR may only pull forward, never past the ceiling) — the widget never paraphrases the clamp; a fixture-equality test pins the on-screen wording to the registered value/rule so what the app *says* is exactly what the engine *does* (§7.12).
6. **Decay is the honest premise, never a threat.** C-035 renders through the T05 `[TRAD]` variant — *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* 5032 / *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim* 791, collection + number + grading, methodology framing, no ruling — placed as the premise row of the no-promise card: recitation slips by nature, so the app guarantees revision. No countdown, timer, urgency copy, red/`color.semantic.error` treatment, or "you'll lose your hifz" phrasing anywhere near it; any forgetting-curve line rendered beside it must itself resolve to a register row via T03 (no hand-written decay fact — the T02 gate enforces this).
7. **The trust-signal card is C-041 → C-045 as calm rows.** C-041 leads (its named sources + the composed [MA] confidence phrase via the E10 `CertaintyLabel`), then C-042, C-043, C-044 as T04 rows; C-045 renders via the T05 variant (*Bukhārī* 1 / *Muslim* 1907, methodology, no ruling, never ranked above the empirical rows as more authoritative). The card *states* the constraint; it must not *become* a badge — no shield/checkmark/award/laurel iconography, no celebratory or emphasis motion, no counter, flat card tokens exactly as supplied by T07's group slot.
8. **Motion and theme are inherited, not added.** Both cards use T07's card treatment across light/sepia/dark; the only motion present is the T04 row's own expansion (reduce-motion handled by the row); this task introduces zero motion, haptics, or emphasis choreography of its own.
9. **Card titles/framing lines are ARB keys only, and non-factual.** e.g. `scienceHonestyCardTitle` / `scienceTrustSignalCardTitle` (final keys per the T09 naming pass); any framing sentence that would assert a fact is forbidden — facts live in rows. Values transcreated in T09 (ckb flagged pending native+scholar review); mixed/Latin runs stay bidi-isolated inside the rows; layout RTL by geometry (`EdgeInsetsDirectional`/`AlignmentDirectional` only, per eng-rtl-and-bidi-layout).
10. **Accessibility inherits from the T04/T05 rows** (one merged localized phrase per row); each card adds a `Semantics` header for its title and nothing else — no card-level announcement that editorializes. Targets and focus order are the rows'; the grade stays text-tag + non-color glyph, never color alone.
11. **Pitfalls to avoid:** rendering any retention %, gauge, or per-page probability; letting C-017's number escape the expansion onto a face; paraphrasing the clamp (drift from §7.6); a threat/countdown/red treatment on the decay ḥadīth; badge-like styling or celebratory motion on the trust-signal card (a gamified "we don't gamify" card is self-refuting); hand-writing a science fact (orphan claim — T02 fails the build); implying the converse of the guarantee ("so your pages are safe") — "safe" language is banned in every form; ranking `[TRAD]` above empirical grades as more authoritative.
