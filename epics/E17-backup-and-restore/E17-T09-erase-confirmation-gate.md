## Goal

The confirmation gate in front of the one truly irreversible action in the app — erase-all-local-data — built as an **honest safeguard, never an obstruction**. Because there is no server and no soft-delete, "erase" actually erases; that truthfulness is the whole design problem. The gate (1) states the **concrete consequence** in plain words — all profiles, cards, and the review history *on this phone* are permanently deleted; because there is no server, **nothing is recoverable elsewhere**; any previously exported `.hifzbackup` file is the only remaining copy — never a bare "Are you sure?"; (2) makes **Cancel / Keep my data the visually-primary action** holding the default focus, low in the thumb band, with the destructive choice a plainer secondary affordance out of easy reach; (3) requires a **second deliberate step sized to the blast radius** (a hold-to-confirm gesture — the *minimum* that defeats a stray tap, never escalated past it); and (4) sits behind the **hard-to-reach top-start corner** entry, using thumb-zone difficulty as a natural safety margin. On confirm it invokes E17-T07's `eraseAllLocalData()` **exactly once** — this task owns no IO. Completion is quiet: no celebratory motion or haptic, no alarming red flourish. The truly-deleted `review_log` (rows gone, no hidden "deleted" flag) is what makes the copy's guarantee honest; a user who means it erases in two clear steps, a user who slipped is caught.

## Implementation notes

This task is presentation-only: no file/DB/key IO, no `HifzBackup` import, no `DateTime.now()`, no navigation. It renders a two-step gate over E10 leaves and dispatches exactly one call into the E17-T07 controller. The consequence copy is **correctness-critical for adab and honesty** → its wording (and the no-fear/no-obstruction invariants) is pinned test-first by widget tests + the banned-phrase lint before the visuals are polished.

1. **Files** (in the `features` umbrella package, `lib/src/settings/`, per eng-add-feature-module; the entry point is hosted by E16's Settings surface): `widgets/erase_confirmation_gate.dart` — the two-step gate composed from the E10 dialog-scaffold leaf, taking callbacks only; `widgets/erase_hold_to_confirm.dart` — the second-step hold control. Both dumb; no state beyond ephemeral step progression.
2. **Step 1 — the consequence dialog.** Title in `type.title`, consequence in `type.body`/`color.text.primary`, stating all four facts concretely: *what* (all profiles, cards, and the review history on this phone), *permanent*, *nothing recoverable elsewhere* (there is no server), *any existing backup file is the only remaining copy*. **Cancel / "Keep my data" is the visually-primary `FilledButton`** holding the default focus, placed low in the thumb band; **"Erase everything" is a plainer outlined/text affordance** toward the sheet's top-start — never the bright, default, or focused button. Never a bare "Are you sure?" (ui-destructive-confirm ¶1–2, ¶4).
3. **Step 2 — sized to the blast radius, and no further.** A whole-device erase warrants one stronger deliberate gesture: a **hold-to-confirm** (per the `template.dart` scaffold) that cannot fire on a single stray tap. Pick this *one* mechanism — do **not** stack hold *and* type-the-word, and do not add a third screen; escalation past the minimum is the obstruction dark pattern (privacy §11; ui-destructive-confirm ¶3). Releasing early cancels harmlessly. Hold progress is conveyed by label + `Semantics` value, never by motion alone, and works identically under reduce-motion.
4. **The copy's honesty bounds.** The guarantee is: rows truly deleted (engineering 05 §2) and, on the encryption flavor, key destruction ⇒ cryptographic unrecoverability (05 §5). The copy therefore never claims physical flash erasure ("shredded", "wiped from the chip"), never says "your data is safe", and never implies a recovery path that doesn't exist.
5. **Confirm wiring.** Completing the hold invokes the injected E17-T07 controller's `eraseAllLocalData()` **exactly once**; the destructive control disables while the call is in flight so a double fire is impossible. The gate performs no navigation — T07's uninitialized state triggers the router guard's redirect. Cancel (either step) closes the gate and calls nothing.
6. **Quiet completion.** At most one light, single `haptic.warning` when step 2 appears (motion 06 §4); **no** success haptic, **no** celebratory motion, **no** alarming red flourish on completion — the result is a calm state change (06 §2; ui-destructive-confirm ¶8). Any enter/exit transition uses `motion.duration.short` + `motion.curve.standard` and degrades to a cross-fade/instant cut under `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`.
7. **Dark-pattern audit.** Walk the gate through all five strategies (privacy §11) and record the pass: two clear steps for a user who means it (no obstruction), honest visual hierarchy (no interface interference), the full consequence stated (no sneaking), no account/backup/unrelated prerequisite (no forced action), asked once (no nagging). The asymmetry protects the *user's* data only, never the app's retention.
8. **Strings via the ARB pipeline, transcreated for fa/ckb/ar** (eng-add-localized-string): every string (title, consequence, "Keep my data", "Erase everything", the hold instruction, the semantic labels) is an `l10n.*` key in `app_ar.arb` (base) + transcreated `fa`/`ckb`; this is sensitive copy → flagged for per-locale native review. Buttons are verbs; no `!`, no emoji; the empathy-then-fact register of voice §4.
9. **Accessibility.** `Semantics` announce the **full consequence** and **which button is destructive** before the user acts; the visible focus ring sits on **Cancel**, never the destructive action; both targets ≥48dp (`touch.min`), ≥8dp apart; the hold control exposes its progress and outcome to screen readers.
10. **RTL by geometry** (eng-rtl-and-bidi-layout): `EdgeInsetsDirectional`/`AlignmentDirectional` only — "top-start" is logical, so the same template serves fa/ckb/ar; ckb's longer copy reflows within the same insets without truncating a load-bearing label.
11. **Pitfalls to avoid:** making the destructive button the bright/default/focused one, or Cancel a greyed text link; a bare "Are you sure?"; stacking a second *and* third gate (obstruction); fear/loss leverage ("you'll lose your hifz") or a command ("you must back up first"); "your data is safe" or any physical-erase claim; forcing a backup export as a gate step; any IO/`HifzBackup`/`DateTime.now()` in the widgets; firing `eraseAllLocalData()` twice; navigating from the gate; confetti or a red alarm on completion; a soft-delete framing ("moved to trash"); hardcoded strings or `left`/`right` insets.

- [ ] The copy stays inside the honesty bounds: truly-deleted framing only — no physical-erase claim, no "your data is safe", no implied recovery path, no soft-delete framing.
- [ ] The five-strategy dark-pattern audit (nagging / obstruction / sneaking / interface interference / forced action) passes and is recorded; no backup/account/unrelated step gates the erase.

## Tests

All deterministic, offline by construction, real bundled fonts, a recording fake for the T07 controller and the haptic boundary (eng-write-dart-test). Test-first on the copy/adab/obstruction invariants.

- `features/test/settings/erase_confirmation_gate_test.dart` (widget) — **written first**:
  - step 1 renders the four-fact consequence and announces it (plus which button is destructive) via `Semantics`;
  - Cancel is the primary `FilledButton` holding the default focus + visible focus ring; the destructive affordance is secondary; both ≥48dp;
  - a single tap on "Erase everything" does **not** erase (the fake controller records zero calls); completing the hold calls `eraseAllLocalData()` **exactly once**; a re-tap while in flight records no second call;
  - Cancel at either step closes the gate with zero controller calls and zero navigation (a recording router observer asserts no push/redirect from the gate).
- `features/test/settings/erase_gate_quiet_completion_test.dart` (widget) — the fake haptic boundary records at most one `haptic.warning` and **no** success/celebration haptic across the whole flow; no confetti/celebration/red-alarm widget appears on completion; under `disableAnimations` the enter/exit transitions are instant and the hold still completes (progress is not motion-conveyed).
- `features/test/settings/erase_gate_banned_phrase_test.dart` (unit) — the never-ship banned-phrase lint over every ARB value this task adds in fa/ckb/ar: guilt/fear/loss leverage ("you'll lose your hifz"), mandate words ("must/should"), "your data is safe", physical-erase claims, soft-delete framing ("trash", "archive"), `!`, emoji; pins that the nothing-recoverable-elsewhere fact is present in each locale's consequence string.
- `features/test/settings/erase_gate_golden_test.dart` (golden) — per-locale (fa/ckb/ar) goldens of step 1 (the Cancel-primary hierarchy and top-start/thumb-band geometry visible) and step 2, on the real bundled fonts, RTL-mirrored by geometry; the ckb-reflow golden proves no truncation.
- Offline guard: the suite runs under the throwing `HttpOverrides` (eng-write-dart-test) — the gate opens no socket. (Erase IO completeness — files + key gone, DB closed first — is E17-T07's `erase_completeness_test.dart`, not re-tested here.)
